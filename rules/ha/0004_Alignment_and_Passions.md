# Babi na 4: Daidaitawa da kuma Kwarewa (Alignment & Passions)

Amfani da Daidaitawa (Alignment) da kuma Kwarewa (Passions) a cikin _Classic Fantasy Imperative_ na iya taimaka wa ’yan wasa da kuma Masu Gudanar da Wasanni (Games Masters) matuka wajen zurfafa yanayin hali, gabatar da hanyoyin labari wadanda suka dogara ga sha’awar hali ko son zuciyarsa, da kuma taimaka wa dan wasa ya fahimci wanda halinsa yake, abin da ke motsa shi, da sauransu. Duk da cewa zaben takamaiman Daidaitawa da Kwarewa da aka samu a wannan babi ya rage ga dan wasa, wasu da aka samu a babi na baya da suka shafi [Ajin](0003_Classes.md) hali ana bukatar su don yin wasa da wancan halin. Babu wani Aji a cikin wannan takarda da ke bukatar takamaiman Daidaitawa ko Kwarewa, amma wasu kamar limamin coci da kuma ɓarawo (wanda ya shiga ƙungiyar ɓarayii), suna buƙatar Rantsuwa ga ƙungiyarsu ko ƙungiyar kasuwanci. Ana iya ɗaukar Rantsuwa kamar Kwarewa dangane da yadda take aiki a cikin wasa.

---
## Daidaitawa (Alignment)

Dole ne kowane hali ya kasance yana da Daidaitawa wanda ya ƙunshi sassa biyu: Dokar Da’a (Ethical Code) da kuma Dokar Halayya (Moral Code). Dokar Da’a tana bayyana matsayi dangane da doka da hargitsi, yayin da Dokar Halayya take ƙayyade ji game da nagarta da mugunta. ’Yan wasa sai su zaɓi abu ɗaya ko biyu masu bayyana kowane ɗayan kuma su ƙayyade Ƙididdigar Farko (Starting Percentages). Misali, Mai Hargitsi (Maras tsari kuma Mai kwadayi) 55%, da Nagari (Mai taimako da Bayarwa) 55%. Ba za ka iya zaɓar Neutral sau biyu ba; maimakon haka, ka yi watsi da Dokar Halayya da Dokar Da’a duka, ka zaɓi True Neutral a matsayin Daidaitawarka guda ɗaya. Dokokin Da’a da Halayya, tare da abubuwan da suke bayyana su, an yi bayani a ƙasa.

#### Dokar Da’a (Ethical Code)

Zaɓi ɗaya daga cikin Lawful (Mai bin doka), Neutral (Tsaka-tsaki), ko Chaotic (Mai hargitsi) a Matsayin Farko na INT+POW+30.

- **Lawful (Mai bin doka)** : Zaɓi ɗaya ko biyu daga cikin abubuwan da suka fi bayyana Dokarka ta Da’a: _Bin al’ada; Mai lissafi; Mai taurin kai; Ƙin Hargitsi; Mai da’a; Mai gaskiya; Mai girma; Mai yanke hukunci; Rashin sassauci; Rashin barkwanci; Biyayya ga ikon da ya dace; Mai tsari; Mai hangen nesa; Mai dogaro; Mai amana; Amintacce; Mai kyawun ɗabi’a._
- **Neutral (Tsaka-tsaki)** : Zaɓi abu ɗaya wanda ya fi bayyana Dokarka ta Da’a: _Yin imanin cewa Doka da Hargitsi son zuciya ne kuma matsanancin yanayi ne; Mai taka-tsantsan; Mai girman kai; Ba ya jin wani abu sosai dangane da Doka da Hargitsi; Mai sassauci; ’Yancin yin aiki; Ba shi da ingantacciyar Dokar Da’a; Mai amfani da hankali; Mai girmama yanayi._
- **Chaotic (Mai hargitsi)** : Zaɓi ɗaya ko biyu daga cikin abubuwan da suka fi bayyana Dokarka ta Da’a: _Maras kyawun ɗabi’a; Mai kishin jini; Mai son sani; Mai rashin amana; Mai yaudara; Maras girma; Maras tsari; Rashin kulawa da ikon da ya dace; Mai son kai; ’Yanci; Ƙin Doka; Maras alhaki; Mai karya doka; Mai girman kai; Mai wasa; Mai saurin fushi; Mai lalatawa; Mai ganganci; Mai zargi; Maras iko; Maras amana; Maras hangen nesa; Maras dogaro._

#### Dokar Halayya (Moral Code)

Zaɓi ɗaya daga cikin Good (Nagari), Neutral (Tsaka-tsaki), ko Evil (Mugun hali) a Matsayin Farko na INT+POW+30.

- **Good (Nagari)** : Zaɓi ɗaya ko biyu daga cikin abubuwan da suka fi bayyana Dokarka ta Halayya: _Abin koyi; Mai son taimakon wasu; Mala'ika; Mai iko; Mai bayarwa; Jin tsoron cutar da marasa laifi; Mai mutunci; Mai gafara; Mai abota; Ƙin Mugunta; Mai taimako; Mai kirki; Son yanayi; Mai rahama; Mai fata; Mai inganci; Mai cin ganyayyaki._
- **Neutral (Tsaka-tsaki)** : Zaɓi abu ɗaya wanda ya fi bayyana Dokarka ta Halayya: _Yin imanin cewa Nagarta da Mugunta son zuciya ne da matsanancin yanayi; Mai taka-tsantsan; Ba ya jin wani abu sosai dangane da Nagarta da Mugunta; Mai sassauci; ’Yancin Aiki; Rashin jajircewa don yin sadaukarwa don kare ko taimaka wa wasu; Jajircewa ga wasu ta hanyar alaƙar sirri, ba Dokar Halayya ba; Ba shi da ingantacciyar Dokar Halayya; Mai amfani da hankali; Kadan yake neman tarayya._
- **Evil (Mugun hali)** : Zaɓi ɗaya ko biyu daga cikin abubuwan da suka fi bayyana Dokarka ta Halayya: _Mai cin zarafi; Mai cin mutum; Mai kwadayi; Mai zalunci; Mai danniya; Jin dadin cutar da marasa laifi; Mai tham’i; Ƙin Nagarta; Mai sha’awa; Mai yaudara; Maras tausayi; Mai girman kai; Mai son azabtarwa; Mai son kai; Maras gaskiya; Mai bautar da mutane; Mai mugun hali; Mai nuna kansa; Mai tashin hankali._

#### True Neutral (Tsaka-tsaki na Gaskiya)

True Neutral yana farawa a Matsayin Farko na INT+POW+30.

- **True Neutral** : A madadin haka, zaɓi ɗaya ko biyu daga cikin abubuwan da suka fi bayyana rashin Dokarka ta Halayya da kuma ta Da’a: _Mai rashin tabbas; Mai taka-tsantsan; Jajircewa ga wasu ta hanyar alaƙar sirri; Mai sassauci; ’Yancin aiki; Mai buɗe ido; Mai amfani da hankali; Mai girmama Yanayi; Yin ƙoƙari don samun daidaito._

## Kwarewa (Passions)

Ana amfani da Kwarewa don taimaka wa ’yan wasa da kuma Masu Gudanar da Wasanni wajen zurfafa yanayin hali, gabatar da hanyoyin labari dangane da sha’awar hali ko son zuciyarsa, da kuma taimaka wa dan wasa ya fahimci wanda halinsa yake, abin da ke motsa shi, da sauransu.

Kwarewa na iya zama don komai – mutum, ƙungiya, manufa, har ma da abu. Kwarewa yawanci ana bayyana su da fi’ili kamar: Sha’awa, Kyama, Lalata, Goyon baya, Tsoro, Gudu, Yin watsi, Ƙi, So, Amincewa da, Kare, Musantawa, Girmamawa, Nema, Rushewa, Azabtarwa, ko Taimakawa.

Ana auna Kwarewa ta hanyar da ake auna fasahohi; ana ba su maki tsakanin 1 da 100 (ko sama da haka). Ana iya inganta Kwarewa ta hanyar da ake inganta fasaha, ko kuma Masu Gudanar da Wasanni su iya ƙara ko rage su dangane da yanayi.

’Yan wasa masu farawa na iya fara wasa da har zuwa Kwarewa guda uku, kowanne bisa ga Halayen guda biyu, tare da ƙarin maki +40 ga na farko, +30 ga na biyu da +20 ga na uku, inda dan wasan yake yanke shawarar tsarin mahimmanci. Waɗannan na iya kasancewa da alaƙa da al'ada, Aji, ko halayen mutum. Misali, Rashin amana (Rogues); Amincewa ga Sarauniya; Son Iyali; Cin nasara kan Hargitsi. Kwarewa suna taka muhimmiyar rawa a cikin Ajin hali da yawa kamar yadda aka yi bayani a babi na gaba. Ga wasu daga cikin waɗannan Ajin, kiyaye Kwarewa a matakin mafi ƙanƙanci wajen aiki ana buƙata don riƙe ƙwarewa na musamman na Aji, kuma a matsanancin yanayi, rasa Kwarewa na iya haifar da rashin Aji.

Ƙimar farko ta Kwarewa ta bambanta bisa ga nau'inta; duba [Teburin Kwarewa](0004_Alignment_and_Passions.md?id=passion-table), a ƙasa.

Wannan sashe yana bayyana kawai abin da Kwarewa suke wakilta da ƙimar su ta asali. Yadda ake amfani da Kwarewa a cikin wasa an tattauna shi a ƙasa.

| Abin Kwarewa | Kashi-kashi na Farko |
| :-- | :-- |
| Mutum, a cikin yanayin soyayya ko dangi | POW+CHA |
| Ƙungiya ko tarin mutane | POW+INT |
| Ƙabila ko nau'in halittu | POW na Hali x2 |
| Wuri | POW+INT na Hali |
| Abu ko jiki | POW na Hali x2 |
| Halayen ɗabi'a | POW+CHA na Hali |

## Amfani da Daidaitawa da Kwarewa

Daidaitawa ko Kwarewa kowace sadaukarwa ce mai zurfi da ke da ikon rinjayar abubuwan da suka faru yayin wasa. Ko da yake ana iya amfani da su a matsayin Fasahohi masu zaman kansu (kiran su fasahohi na iya zama rashin adalci gare su), ana ƙara faɗaɗa Kwarewa a ƙasa don bayyana dalla-dalla yadda suke aiki da yadda za su iya rinjayar haruffan _Classic Fantasy Imperative_ da yaƙe-yaƙe.

A taƙaice, ana iya amfani da Daidaitawa da Kwarewa kamar haka:

- Don haɓaka wata fasaha, yana nuna zurfin ji na mutum da yadda yake motsa aiki. Lokacin da aka yi amfani da shi ta wannan hanya, Daidaitawa ko Kwarewa na ƙara 20% na ƙimarta ga fasahar da ake amfani da ita, idan haɓakar tana da mahimmanci a fannin jigo da wasan kwaikwayo.
- A matsayin Ikon motsa zaɓuka, sha’awa da ayyukan motsin rai da martani. Lokacin da aka yi amfani da shi ta wannan hanya, ana yin juyi na yau da kullun (Standard roll) akan Daidaitawa ko Kwarewa don tantance yadda halin yake tunani da ji game da wani abu. Idan juyin ya yi nasara, to halin yana aiki daidai da abin da Daidaitawa ko Kwarewa za ta bayar da umarni. Idan juyin ya gaza, to halin na iya yin aiki ba tare da jin an tauye shi ta hanyar motsin Kwarewa ba.
- Don kalubalantar wasu Daidaitawa ko Kwarewa – har ma da waɗanda guda ɗaya halin ke da su. Yawanci ana amfani da wannan inda Daidaitawa zai iya yin karo da Kwarewa, ko ma lokacin da Kwarewa biyu za su iya yin karo. Misali, ƙaunar mutum na iya bayar da umarnin wani aikin da zai saba wa Rantsuwa ko amincin. Anan yi amfani da Juyi na Karo (Opposed Roll) tsakanin Daidaitawa da Kwarewa, ko Kwarewa biyu, inda wanda ya fi nasara ke tantance yadda halin yake aiki.
- A matsayin ma'aunin zurfin sadaukarwa, imani, da aminci ga wata manufa. Yadda ƙimar Daidaitawa ko Kwarewa take da yawa, haka halin yake da sadaukarwa. Haruffa masu kama da Daidaitawa ko Kwarewa na iya kwatanta ƙimarsu don tantance wanda ke nuna zurfin sadaukarwa.
- Don tsayayya da wasu nau'ikan sarrafa tunani ko mamayar sihiri. A wasu lokuta inda ake tilasta wa hali yin aikin da ya saba wa Daidaitawarsu ko Kwarewarsu, za su iya maye gurbin kowane ɗayan da Ƙarfin Zuciya (Willpower) a cikin Juyi na Karo.

Ƙimar Daidaitawa na hali na iya raguwa yayin wasa idan halin ya ci gaba da yin watsi da ɗabi’arsa da/ko da’a da aka zaɓa. Idan ya faɗi ƙasa da Matsayin Farko na halin, zai canza zuwa Daidaitawa mai karo, misali daga Nagari zuwa Tsaka-tsaki ko Mugun hali. Masu Gudanar da Wasanni ya kamata su tantance sabon Daidaitawa da Matsayi, dangane da yanayin. Canjin Daidaitawa na iya haifar da rasa damar yin Ikon Aji. Duba rubutun Ajin da ya dace don cikakken bayani.

Ana iya haɓaka sabbin Kwarewa a kowane lokaci yayin zaman wasan _Classic Fantasy Imperative_ idan yanayin ya ba da damar hakan. Mutum na iya haɓaka 'Ƙin X' nan take inda wani ya yi wani abu da zai haifar da ƙiyayya mai ƙarfi. Ubangiji ko shugaban da ke kira ga Rantsuwa zai nan da nan kafa Kwarewa ta 'Amincewa da X' da zarar an ɗauki ko an rantse wannan Rantsuwar – wani abu da aka ƙara bincike a cikin sashen Daidaitawa/Rantsuwa na kowane [Aji](0003_Classes.md).

Sabbin Kwarewa da aka haɓaka yayin wasa ta wannan hanya ba sa kashe Juyin Kwarewa (Experience Rolls) kuma ana kafa su nan take. Tabbas, ’yan wasa kuma na iya zaɓar kafa Kwarewa yayin ingantawa, tare da farashin Juyin Kwarewa don samun Kwarewa a Matsayin Asalinta: duba sassan Daidaitawa da Kwarewa daban-daban a cikin babi na [Al'ada da Ƙabilu](0002_Culture_and_Races.md).

## Zurfafawa da Ragewa

Daidaitawa, Kwarewa, da Rantsuwa na iya ƙaruwa yayin wasa ba tare da dogara ga Juyin Kwarewa ba, dangane da ƙarfin duk abin da ya faru don haifar da ƙaruwar. Haka kuma, suna iya, kuma suna raguwa. Imani mai ƙarfi na iya girgiza ta abubuwa da yawa, wanda ke haifar da raguwar wannan Kwarewa ko, a cikin yanayi mafi tsanani, juyawa gaba ɗaya.

Misali, hali mai 'Amincewa da Shugaba' na iya, idan shugabansa ya yi aiki da yaudara, ko dai ya rage Kwarewarsa ko ya juya ta zuwa 'Rashin amana da Shugaba' a iri ɗaya kashi-kashi. Masu Gudanar da Wasanni yana bukatar ya tantance ko Kwarewa tana raguwa ko tana juyawa. Idan ta ragu, to tana yin hakan ne bisa ga ƙarfin tushen canjin.

Teburin Zurfafawa da Ragewa yana nuna yadda Kwarewa take canzawa.

| Ƙarfi | 1d10+10 |
