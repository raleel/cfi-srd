# Classic Fantasy Imperative System Resource Document

Siga 1.0, Satumba 2023

_Classic Fantasy Imperative_ wani tsarin wasa ne a bude, wanda yake karkashin lasisin ORC, kuma sigar wasan _Mythras_ ne, wanda ke mai da hankali kan binciken kurkuku, kasada mai kayatarwa, da kuma fuskantar hadurra masu ban tsoro da suka shafi dukiyar da aka boye, sihiri, da kuma makiya masu hadari. _Classic Fantasy Imperative_ yana koyi da nishadin wasannin taka rawa na farko da aka taba yi a tarihi, amma yana gabatar da su ta fuskar tsarin wasan _Mythras_, wanda yake amfani da 1d100 (ko kuma kashi dari), maimakon amfani da d20.

Wannan takarda tana ba da cikakkun dokoki—amma ba na karshe ba—wadanda suka hada da kirkirar hali, kabilun halaye da aji, kwarewa, dokoki don wasu yanayi na musamman, yaki, da kuma sihiri – wadanda suka wadatar don ba 'yan wasa da kuma Jagoran Wasa (Games Masters) damar farawa, kuma su zama ginshiki mai karfi don ci gaban gaba. _Classic Fantasy Imperative_ ya dace gaba daya da _Classic Fantasy Imperative_, cikakkun dokokin _Mythras_, da kuma _Mythras Imperative_. Bugu da kari, ya kuma dace da sauran tsarin wasannin da suke amfani da kashi dari karkashin lasisin ORC.

## Laisin ORC

An wallafa _Classic Fantasy Imperative_ ta amfani da Laisin ORC. Laisin ORC yana ba da hanya ga wadanda suka kirkiro wasanni don su raba fasahar wasanninsu a bude kuma yana ba wa masu amfani a nan gaba damar yin amfani da su, gyarawa, da kuma sake raba gyare-gyaren wadannan fasahohin wasan kyauta.

Abin da ake rabawa a bude karkashin ORC ana kiransa **Abubuwan da aka ba da lasisi (Licensed Material)**, kuma ya hada da manyan sassan wasan, kamar bayanan kwarewa (statblocks), dokokin wasa, halayen hali, da hanyoyi da tsarin da ke tattare da buga wasan, da kuma duk wani abu da mai lasisin yake son rabawa. Laisin din yana da fadi kuma yana ba ka damar amfani da Abubuwan da aka ba da lasisi a duniya baki daya tare da littattafan da aka buga, wasannin bidiyo, kwasfan fayiloli (podcasts), AI, ko wata fasaha da za ta iya wanzuwa a nan gaba. Yawancin abubuwan da aka samu a cikin _Classic Fantasy Imperative_ sun kunshi Abubuwan da aka ba da lasisi.

Abin da ba a rabawa karkashin ORC ana kiransa **Abubuwan da aka kebe (Reserved Material)** kuma ya hada da alamar kasuwanci, tarihin duniyar wasan, labaru, halaye na musamman, da fasahar gani. Mun lissafta Abubuwan da aka kebe a cikin Sanarwar ORC, a kasa.

Karkashin Laisin ORC, za ka iya amfani, raba, daidaitawa, da kuma ginawa a kan Abubuwan da aka ba da lasisi yadda kake so, matukar ka ba da sahihin bayanin tushen aiki a matsayin sanarwa a cikin aikinka da aka buga. Wannan sanarwa tana ba da daraja ga wadanda suka bayar da lasisin farko da kuma gudunmawarsu ga aikin, tare da ba wa masu amfani a nan gaba hanya mai sauki don ba da daraja ga marubucin aikin da sanarwar take ciki. Ta hanyar amfani da kayan da aka bayar da lasisi karkashin ORC, kana amincewa kai tsaye don bayar da lasisin fasahar wasanka a cikin aikinka da aka buga karkashin wadannan sharuddan. Idan ka kirkiro naka Abubuwan da aka kebe, to su ne dukiyarka ta kashin kai.

## Sabo da d100?

_Classic Fantasy Imperative_ tsari ne na d100 ko kashi dari. Ana amfani da kashi dari, ko 1d100, don magance mahimman ayyuka – amfani da kwarewa, yaki, jefa sihiri, da sauran su. Tsari ne na yin birgima a kasa da adadi, wanda ke nufin cewa adadin da aka sa gaba ana bayyana shi azaman kashi dari (misali, Athletics 65%), kuma duka Jagoran Wasa da dan wasa suna neman birgima (roll) daidai ko kasa da wannan adadin da aka sa gaba, ta amfani da 1d100, don samun Nasara. Yana da sauki sosai a maye gurbin 1d100 da d20 idan aka fi so, kuma duk kashi dari za a iya raba su da 5 (a zagaye sama) don samun adadin da aka sa gaba don birgimar d20. Athletics 65% zai zama Athletics 13, a misalinmu. Koyaya, 1d100 yana ba da babban sassauci da kuma zurfin fahimta wanda yake ginshikin dukan wasan, don haka shawararmu ita ce a gwada hanyar 1d100 da farko. Duk da cewa yana iya zama kamar sabanin hankali a bukaci a birgima _kasa_ da wani abu (a wasanni da yawa, mafi girma yawanci shi ne mafi kyau!), samun adadin da aka sa gaba da aka bayyana a matsayin kewayon kashi dari wanda mutum yake bukatar ya birgima _a ciki_ yana sa shi sauki da kuma kaskanci don ganin damarka na nasara, kuma yana ba da damar wasu 'dabarun birgima,' kamar Birgima da aka fafata (Opposed Rolls), Nasarar Koli (Criticals), da Kuskure (Fumbles), da wasu abubuwa da dama da aka bincika daga baya a cikin wadannan dokoki. Wasan taka rawa na kashi dari ya dade yana wanzuwa kuma ingantaccen tsarin wasa ne tare da ingantacciyar fasahar asali wadda take mai sassauci, fahimtarwa, kuma tana saurin bace cikin bango yayin wasa.

## Saukakawa

Masu mallakar _Mythras_ da _Classic Fantasy_ na dogon lokaci za su lura cewa a wasu wurare an saukaka dokokin _Classic Fantasy Imperative_. An yi hakan ne domin Jagoran Wasa ya iya gabatar da wasan ga sabbin 'yan wasa da sauri tare da karancin damuwa. Wani yanki inda _Classic Fantasy Imperative_ ya bambanta shi ne a cikin hada ma'aunin Imperial maimakon na metric. Hadawa guda biyu yana cinye sarari da yawa kuma zai bukaci tebura da yawa yayin mu'amala da Makamai na nesa da makamantansu. An zabi tsarin Imperial ne kawai don saukaka amfani da tabarmar yaki (battle mats) marasa adadi da suka riga suka wanzu, kuma don sanya sauya kasada daga wasu tsarin wasanni da ke amfani da ma'aunin Imperial su zama masu sauki. Lokacin da kake cike Takardar Hali (Character Sheet) taka, ji dadin amfani da Teburorin Sauya Ma'auni a cikin Shafin Karin Bayani (Appendix), da duk wani tsarin ma'auni da ka ga ya fi amfani da kuma saba da shi.

## Zagayawa Lambobi da Sakamako

A wasu lokutan za a bukaci ka raba lambobi - yawanci darajar kwarewa (kamar don tantance Nasarar Koli, wanda yake 1/10th na darajar kwarewar). Duk lokacin da sakamakon rabo ya haifar da juzu'i (fraction), koyaushe ka zagaya sama zuwa cikakkiyar lamba. Don haka, misali, 1/10th na 64% shi ne 6.4; wannan ana zagaya shi sama zuwa 7.

## Zaratan da aka yi amfani da su a cikin Wasan

_Classic Fantasy Imperative_ yana amfani da daidaitaccen saitin zarata (dice) na polyhedral: d4, d6, d8, d10, d12, d20 da d100. Bugu da kari, ana bukatar zaratan masu zuwa:

- **d2:** Birgima kowane irin zarata. Idan lamba ce mara biyu (odd) to 1 ce, idan kuma lamba ce mai biyu (even) to 2 ce. Ko kuma ka jefa kudin kwabo.

- **d3:** Birgima zarata mai bangare shida; 1-2 = 1, 3-4 = 2, 5-6 = 3.
