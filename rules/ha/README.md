# Classic Fantasy Imperative: Takardar Albarkatun Tsarin

Sigar 1.0, Satumba 2023

_Classic Fantasy Imperative_ wani nau'i ne na buɗe ido, mai lasisin ORC, na tsarin dokokin _Mythras_, wanda ke mai da hankali kan binciken kurkuku, babban kasada, da kuma yanayi masu tashin hankali da suka shafi dukiya, sihiri, da kuma makiya masu haɗari. _Classic Fantasy Imperative_ tana kwaikwayon jin daɗin wasan kwaikwayo na farko da aka taɓa yi a harkar, amma tana gabatar da shi ta fuskar tsarin wasan _Mythras_, wanda yake amfani da 1d100 (ko kashi), maimakon amfani da d20.

Wannan takarda tana ba da cikakken bayani, kodayake ba na ƙarshe ba, na dokoki, gami da ƙirƙirar hali, ƙabilun hali da azuzuwan, basira, dokoki don wasu yanayi na yau da kullun, yaƙi, da sihiri – fiye da isa ga ƴan wasa da kuma Jagororin Wasa (Games Masters) su fara, kuma su samar da ingantaccen tushe don ƙarin ci gaba. _Classic Fantasy Imperative_ tana dacewa gaba ɗaya tare da _Classic Fantasy Imperative_, dokokin asali na _Mythras_, da _Mythras Imperative_. Ƙari ga haka, tana kuma dacewa da wasu tsarin wasan kashi da ke da lasisin ORC.

## Lasisin ORC

_Classic Fantasy Imperative_ an buga ta ne ta amfani da Lasisin ORC. Lasisin ORC yana ba da hanya ga waɗanda suka ƙirƙiri wasanni don raba makanikai (mechanics) na wasansu a fili kuma yana ba masu amfani damar amfani da su kyauta, gyara su, da sake raba daidaitawar waɗannan makanikan wasan.

Abin da ake rabawa a fili ƙarƙashin ORC an san shi da **Abubuwan da aka Ba da Lasisi (Licensed Material)**, kuma ya haɗa da manyan abubuwan wasan, kamar bayanan ƙididdiga (statblocks), dokokin wasa, halayen hali, da hanyoyi da tsarin da ke tattare da yin wasan, gami da duk wani abu da mai lasisin ke son rabawa a fili. Lasisin yana da faɗi kuma yana ba ku damar amfani da Abubuwan da aka Ba da Lasisi a duniya baki ɗaya dangane da littattafan da aka buga, wasannin bidiyo, faifan sauti (podcasts), AI, ko kowace fasaha da za ta iya wanzuwa ko za a iya ƙirƙira a nan gaba. Yawancin abubuwan da aka samu a _Classic Fantasy Imperative_ sun ƙunshi Abubuwan da aka Ba da Lasisi.

Abin da ba a rabawa ƙarƙashin ORC an san shi da **Abubuwan da aka Keɓe (Reserved Material)** kuma ya haɗa da alamun kasuwanci (trademarks), tarihin duniya, labarai, haruffa na musamman, da fasahar gani. Mun lissafa Abubuwan da aka Keɓe a cikin Sanarwar ORC, a ƙasa.

Ƙarƙashin Lasisin ORC kuna iya amfani da, raba, daidaita, da kuma gina kan Abubuwan da aka Ba da Lasisi yadda kuka ga dama, muddin kuka ba da yabo da ya dace a cikin aikin da kuka buga. Wannan sanarwa tana ba da yabo ga waɗanda suka ba da lasisin kuma tana ba da hanya mai sauƙi ga masu amfani su yaba wa marubucin aikin da sanarwar ta bayyana. Ta hanyar amfani da kayan da aka ba da lasisi ƙarƙashin ORC, kun amince ta atomatik don ba da lasisin makanikan wasanku a cikin aikin da kuka buga ƙarƙashin sharuɗɗa ɗaya. Idan kun haɓaka Abubuwan da aka Keɓe naku, wannan mallakarku ne na musamman.

## Sabo da d100?

_Classic Fantasy Imperative_ wani tsarin d100 ko kashi ne. Ana amfani da dice na kashi, ko 1d100, don magance mahimman ayyuka – amfani da basira, yaƙi, jefa sihiri, da sauransu. Tsari ne na juye-kasa (roll-under), ma'ana cewa lambar da aka nufa ana bayyana ta azaman kashi (misali, Athletics 65%), kuma duka Jagoran Wasa da ɗan wasa suna neman jefa lamba daidai ko ƙasa da wannan lambar da aka nufa, ta amfani da 1d100, don samun Nasara. Yana da sauƙi sosai a maye gurbin 1d100 da d20 idan aka fi so, kuma duk kashi za a iya raba su da 5 (zagaye sama) don samun lambar da aka nufa don jefa d20. Athletics 65% zai zama Athletics 13, a cikin misalinmu. Koyaya, 1d100 yana ba da sassauci da ma'ana da yawa waɗanda ke goyan bayan dukan wasan, don haka shawararmu ita ce a gwada hanyar 1d100 da farko. Yayin da zai iya zama kamar ba a so a jefa *ƙasa* da wani abu ba (a wasanni da yawa, mafi girma yawanci mafi kyau ne!), samun lambar da aka nufa da aka bayyana azaman kewayon kashi wanda mutum yake buƙatar jefa *a ciki* yana sa ya zama sauƙi kuma a bayyane a ga damar samun nasararka, kuma yana ba da damar 'dabarun dice' da yawa, kamar Juyi na Musamman (Opposed Rolls), Mahimman Nasara (Criticals), da Kuskure (Fumbles), da wasu abubuwa kaɗan da aka bincika daga baya a cikin waɗannan dokokin. Wasan kwaikwayo na kashi ya daɗe yana wanzuwa kuma tabbataccen tsarin wasa ne tare da ingantaccen makaniki na asali wanda yake da sauƙi, fahimta, kuma yakan shuɗe a baya yayin wasa.

## Sauƙaƙewa

Masu mallakar _Mythras_ da _Classic Fantasy_ na dogon lokaci za su lura cewa a wasu wuraren an sauƙaƙe dokokin _Classic Fantasy Imperative_. An yi hakan ne domin Jagoran Wasa ya iya saurin gabatar da wasan ga sabbin ƴan wasa tare da ƙarancin hayaniya. Wani yanki inda _Classic Fantasy Imperative_ ya bambanta shi ne wajen haɗa ma'aunin Imperial maimakon na metric. Haɗa duka biyun yana cinye sarari da yawa kuma zai buƙaci tebura da yawa lokacin ma'amala da Makamai masu nisa da makamantansu. An zaɓi tsarin Imperial ne kawai don sauƙaƙe amfani da ƙarin ma'aunin yaƙi (battle mats) da yawa da suka riga suka wanzu, kuma don sa sauya kasada daga wasu tsarin wasan da ke amfani da ma'aunin Imperial ya zama mafi sauƙi. Lokacin cike Takardar Halinku, jin daɗin amfani da Teburan Sauyawa a cikin Shafi (Appendix), da duk tsarin ma'aunin da kuka fi amfani da shi kuma kuka saba da shi.

## Zagaye Lambobi da Sakamako

A wasu lokuta za a buƙaci ku raba lambobi - yawanci ƙimar da ke cikin basira (kamar don ƙayyade Nasarar Mahimmanci (Critical Success), wanda shine 1/10 na ƙimar basirar). Duk lokacin da sakamakon rarrabuwa ya haifar da ɓangare (fraction), koyaushe ku zagaye sama zuwa lamba gaba ɗaya. Don haka, misali, 1/10 na 64% shine 6.4; wannan ana zagaye shi sama zuwa 7.

## Dice da aka yi amfani da su a Wasan

_Classic Fantasy Imperative_ tana amfani da saitin dice na polyhedral na yau da kullun: d4, d6, d8, d10, d12, d20 da d100. Bugu da ƙari, ana buƙatar dice masu zuwa:

- **d2:** Jefa kowane dice. Lamba mara ma'ana (odd number) tana daidai da 1 kuma mai ma'ana (even) tana daidai da 2. Ko jefa tsabar kuɗi.

- **d3:** Jefa dice mai gefe shida; 1-2 = 1, 3-4 = 2, 5-6 = 3.
