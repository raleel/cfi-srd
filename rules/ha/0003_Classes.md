# Sura na 3: Aji

Kowane hali na _Classic Fantasy Imperative_ yana fara wasa ne bayan ya sami horo a cikin wani Aji. Wannan Aji shi ne aikin da halin ya shiga a matsayin wani ɓangare na sauyawa zuwa girma, kuma yana ƙara fayyace yadda basirar halin ke haɓaka.

’Yan wasa suna da 'yancin zaɓar Aji da ya dace da launin fata da al'adunsu, muddin Jagoran Wasa (Games Master) ya ga ya dace. Bayanin da aka bayar yana ba da wasu jagorori game da launukan fata waɗanda galibi suke sha'awar wannan Aji. Waɗannan shawarwari ba su ne na ƙarshe ba kuma ana iya canza su ta hanyar zaɓin ƴan wasa, ko kuma don dacewa da saitunan wasa masu ban mamaki. Misali, a cikin yaƙin neman zaɓe da aka gina a kan duniyar hamada da ke mutuwa, inda mutanen halfling suke ƙabilu ne masu cin naman mutane, ba zai zama abin mamaki ba idan aka ba su damar zama berserkers. Bai kamata ’yan wasa su kalli waɗannan ƙuntatawa na launin fata/al'adu a matsayin dokoki masu ƙarfi ba, sai dai a matsayin wuraren farawa na yau da kullum. Ya kamata ƴan wasa su ji ’yancin ba da shawarar madadin ga Jagoran Wasa, wanda shi kuma, zai iya ba da izinin haɗin gwiwa na Launin Fata da Aji da ba a saba gani ba don dacewa da saitin wasa na musamman ko ba da lada ga labarin baya na ƙirƙira.

#### Tsarin Matsayin Aji

Tsarin Aji da Matsayi na _Classic Fantasy Imperative_ ya dogara ne akan ƙungiyoyi daban-daban kamar su Odar (Orders) ko Guilds. Misali, Guild na Mayaka (Fighter's Guild), Odar Limamai (Clerical Order), Guild na Masu Sihiri (Magic-User's Guild), da Guild na Barayi (Thieves' Guild).

Ana amfani da Matsayi a matsayin kayan aiki don jagorantar haɓakar hali kuma yawancin tsarin Aji an shirya su ne a cikin matsayi mai siffar dala. Mambobi na yau da kullum (Matsayi 0) su ne tushen dala kuma su ne mambobi mafi yawa a cikin Aji.

Matsayi mafi girma (Matsayi 1–5) suna wakiltar mambobi ƙwararru na Ajin, waɗanda ke aiki cikakken lokaci a ƙarƙashin dokokinsu.

Adadin yana raguwa yayin da mutum yake ci gaba ta hanyar matsayi har sai ƙalilan, ko ma ɗaya kacal, su mamaye matakan sama. Ci gaba yana kawo babban gata, daraja, ilimi, kuma wani lokacin, sihiri, ko wasu fa'idodi.

#### Ƙaruwa a Matsayi

Halaye suna neman tashi cikin matsayi don neman babban girmamawa, suna, ko nauyi, ko kawai saboda ƙishirwar ilimi da iko.

Kowane Aji yana buƙatar basirar Aji da yawa a takamaiman matakin don Ci gaba. Lokacin da aka fara ƙirƙirar halin, waɗannan basira za su ƙayyade Matsayin farko na halin. Samun kowace basirar Aji 5 a 40% ko fiye zai sanya halin a Matsayi 1, tun da yake yana nuna cewa mutumin yana da iyawa kuma ya cancanci Ikon musamman na wancan Matsayin. In ba haka ba, halin yana farawa a Matsayi 0. Lokacin da halin ya kai Matsayi 1 a ƙarshe, duk Ikon da ba a fayyace a matsayin mai Matsayi ba, ana iya samun su ba tare da tsada a cikin Jijjiga Gwaninta (Experience Rolls) ba, tunda ana iya ɗauka cewa halin ya kasance yana aiwatar da waɗannan Ikon a wasa. Ikon da ke da Matsayi ana iya siyan su akai-akai tare da Jijjiga Gwaninta da zarar an kai Matsayi 1.

Lokacin da aka kai matakin gwaninta mafi girma, bisa ga Basirar halin, halin yana ƙara Matsayi kuma. A wancan lokacin ana iya koyon Ikon da sihiri da aka ƙima a wancan Matsayin, tunda halin ya nuna matakin fahimta da gwaninta da ake buƙata.

Galibi, idan halin yana da kyakkyawar alaƙa da kowane Rantsuwa da Ajin ke buƙata kuma yana da adadin basirar Aji da ake buƙata a matakin da ake buƙata, halin zai ƙaru ta atomatik zuwa Matsayin da ya dace. Kowane Matsayi sama da 1 kuma yana ba da damar halaye su ƙara wani Maki na Sa’a (Luck Point) zuwa jimlar su, yana ba su kyakkyawar dama ta tsira daga fafatawa mafi tsauri ba tare da buƙatar ƙara Maki na Buga (Hit Points) na wucin gadi ba. Kowane Aji yana da tebur wanda ke bayyana buƙatun basira a kowane Matsayi, da kuma taƙaitaccen bayani game da duk wani Hazaka da/ko Ikon da aka ƙima da ake da su. Abin da ke biyo baya yana ba da bayanin kowane abu a cikin waɗannan teburan:

- **Matsayi (Rank):** Wakilin lambobi na Matsayin halin.
- **Taken (Title):** Taken da aka ba wa halin mai Matsayin da ya dace.
- **Ci gaba (Advancement):** Adadin basirar aji da matakin basirar da ake buƙata don samun Matsayin da ya dace. Ana iya zaɓar waɗannan daga kowane ɗayan basirar aji da aka lissafa. Babu buƙatar zaɓar su a gaba. Basirar da ke karɓar ƙarin +40, kamar Al'adu da Harsunan Asali, ba sa ƙidaya don ci gaba.
- **Sihiri a Kwakwalwa (Spells in Memory):** Idan ya dace, wannan yana nuna nawa sihiri na kowane Matsayi da kake da shi a ƙwaƙwalwarka.
- **Maki na Buga (Hit Points):** Wasu matsayi suna ba da ƙarin Maki na Buga, wanda ake ƙarawa zuwa duk wurare. Darajar da aka lura ta haɗa da duk ƙarin fa'idodin da suka gabata.
- **Maki na Sa’a (Luck Points):** Kowane Matsayi bayan na farko yana ba wa halin ƙarin Maki na Sa’a 1. Darajar da aka lura ta haɗa da duk ƙarin fa'idodin da suka gabata.
- **Maki na Aiki (Action Points):** Wasu matsayi suna ba da ƙarin Maki na Aiki. Darajar da aka lura ta haɗa da duk ƙarin fa'idodin da suka gabata.

## Limami (Cleric)

Limamai su ne ɓangaren yaƙi na odar addini kuma suna da gwaninta wajen kare addininsu da mabiyansa. A lokutan zaman lafiya, limamai suna yawo a ƙasar suna yada kalmar allolinsu da neman waɗanda za su musulunta (ko komawa ga addininsu). Limamai suna da albarkar ikon yin sihiri ta hanyar allolin addininsu kuma su ne ƙwararru a yaƙi da waɗanda suka mutu (undead), suna da ikon kora su da addu'a kawai.

#### Jinsin/Al'ada na Yau da Kullum

Kowa.

#### Halaye Masu Muhimmanci

STR, INT, POW, da CHA

#### Bukatun Daidaitawa/Rantsuwa

- Daidaitawa: Kowa, kamar yadda ya dace da gumaka ko alloli.
- Rantsuwar Limanci 30% da kuma INT+POW na halin.

Duba [Sura ta 4: Daidaitawa da Sha'awa](0004_Alignment_and_Passions.md).


Baya ga Daidaitawa da Sha'awa na launin fata/al'adu, duk limamai suna rantsar da Rantsuwar Halayyar kirki ga odar su ta limanci da allahn ko gumakan da odar take girmamawa. Wani ɓangare na Rantsuwar yawanci ya haɗa da ba da zakka na 10% na duk dukiya ga odar. Inda gumaka suke, za a yi amfani da Daidaitawar halin don taimakawa wajen ƙayyade waɗanne alloli na gumakan da ake so za a bauta wa. Yawanci, oda za ta sadaukar da kansu ga duk allolin wani takamaiman gumaka da ke da Sha'awa iri ɗaya. Limami yawanci yana daidaita da duk allolin wani takamaiman gumaka tare da Daidaitawa da ba ta saɓawa nasu ba. Misali, limami mai Daidaitawar kirki zai yi hidima kuma ya girmama duk alloli masu kirki da na tsakiya na gumakansu. Suna iya yin aiki da waɗancan alloli masu mugun hali, ko ma su yarda da su kuma su girmama su, amma ba za su ba su bauta ba. Limami mai Gaskiya na Tsakiya (True Neutral) zai iya zaɓar alloli masu kirki ko mugunta tare da waɗancan masu halin tsakiya idan ana so amma ba zai iya zaɓar duka biyun ba.

#### Basirar Aji

- **Basira ta Yau da Kullum:** Basirar Yaƙi (Limami), Taimakon Farko, Tasiri, Basira, Wuri, Waƙa, Ƙarfin Zuciya
- **Basirar Ƙwararru:** Limami yana karɓar Channel da Ibada (takamaiman gumaka ko allahn) ta atomatik, da ƙarin basira 3 daga waɗannan: Bureaucracy, Ladabi, Al'adu (kowane), Warkarwa, Ilimin (Alchemy), Ilimin (Addini), Harshe (kowane) ko Ilimin (kowane), da Oratory.

#### Ikon Limami na Sanya Sulke da Makamai

Limamai suna da gwaninta da kowane nau'in sulke (haske da nauyi), kuma da duk garkuwa (ban da garkuwar hasumiya). Sihirin Limanci na Allah ba ya shafar sanya sulke. Basirarsu ta Yaƙi ta haɗa da amfani da duk makamai na yau da kullum. Limamai na musamman na iya samun damar amfani da makamai daban-daban a matsayin ɓangare na Basirarsu ta Yaƙi. Wannan ya wuce iyakar wannan takaddar duk da haka Jagoran Wasa na iya amfani da wannan bayanin idan sun yanke shawara.

### Tsarin Matsayi

Duk limamai ana kimanta su ta Matsayi, wanda shi ne ƙimar iko da gwaninta. Lokacin da aka sami takamaiman matakin gwaninta a cikin ƙayyadadden adadin basirar Aji, limamai na iya ƙara Matsayinsu kamar yadda suka nuna matakin cancantar da ake buƙata. Lokacin da aka kai wannan matakin cancantar, ana samun ƙarin Maki na Sa’a kuma ana iya samun sabon Ikon. Ana samun wannan bayanin a kan tebur mai zuwa a ƙarƙashin Ci gaba. Idan kun cika abubuwan da ake buƙata, halin ku yana farawa a matsayin Matsayi 1 Mai Fara (Initiate) tare da Ikon da ya dace da aka lura a ƙasa; in ba haka ba, kuna farawa a matsayin Matsayi 0 Mamba na Yau da Kullum (Lay Member) tare da Ikon yin sihiri na Matsayi 0, amma babu wani Ikon.

#### Ikon Farawa

Limami ya san duk Ikon da ke biyo baya a Matsayi 1. Bugu da ƙari, yayin ƙirƙirar hali, zaka iya zaɓar Ikon Matsayi 1 ɗaya kyauta idan kun cika buƙatunsa.

- **Mai Sihiri:** Limamai su ne ƙwararrun sihiri na Allah, suna iya yin sihiri na warkarwa, tsaro, da kariya don taimaka wa abokan gaba. Duk da haka, ba su rasa sihiri mai ƙarfi na kai hari don dukan waɗanda suke tsayayya da abubuwan da suka dace da su ba. Ba kamar masu amfani da sihiri ba, limamai ba sa buƙatar littafin sihiri, suna da damar amfani da duk sihiri na Matsayin su ko ƙasa. Yin sihiri yana buƙatar 1 ko fiye da Ayyukan Jefa Sihiri waɗanda suka ƙunshi ƴan gajerun addu'o'i, motsin jiki tare da alamar su ta tsarki, da nasarar jefa Channel. Adadin sihiri na Matsayin su na yanzu da limami zai iya samu a ƙwaƙwalwarsa ana samunsa a [Teburin Matsayin Limami](0003_Classes.md?id=cleric-rank-table) a sama a ƙarƙashin Sihiri a Kwakwalwa.
- **Sihiri na Farawa:** Limami yana farawa da damar amfani da duk sihiri na limami na Matsayi 0 da duk wasu na Matsayin su ko ƙasa da haka. Duba [Jerin Sihirin Limami](0003_Classes.md?id=cleric-spell-list). Ba su san su ba a zahiri, kamar yadda mai amfani da sihiri 'ya san' sihiri, amma suna iya yin addu'a don kowane ɗayansu lokacin zaɓar waɗanne sihiri za su sa a ƙwaƙwalwa. Adadin sihiri da limami zai iya sa a ƙwaƙwalwa a kowane Matsayi ana samunsa a Teburin Matsayin limami a sama. Duba [Sa Sihiri a Kwakwalwa](0009_Magic.md?id=memorizing-spells) don ƙarin bayani.
- **Kora Undead:** Limamai suna iya korar waɗanda suka mutu, suna kore su ta hanyar imani kawai. [Korar Undead](0003_Classes.md?id=turning-undead) an tattauna shi dalla-dalla a ƙasa.

#### Ikon da ke da Matsayi

Kamar yadda sunan ya nuna, Ikon da ke da Matsayi ana iya zaɓar su ne kawai ta halin da yake da Matsayi ko sama da haka. Yawancin suna da Basirar Buƙata (Prerequisite Skill) wanda dole ne ya kasance a wani matakin basira ko sama da haka. Ba a kulle ku daga Ikon da suka gabata ba lokacin da aka kai sabon Matsayi. Idan ka kasa ɗaukar Ikon Matsayi 2 misali, za ka iya komawa ka ɗauke shi a Matsayi 3 ko ma daga baya. Ba a yarda a ɗauki fiye da Ikon da ke da Matsayi ɗaya a kowane zama ba.

#### Matsayi 1:

Don kowane kashe 1 na Jijjiga Gwaninta za ka iya samun ɗaya daga cikin waɗannan Ikon (ɗaya a kowane zama, kuma kowane Ikon ana iya ɗauka sau ɗaya kawai):

- **Babban Maida Hankali (Powerful Concentration):** Yana buƙatar Ibada na 50% ko fiye. Jarabawar Ƙarfin Zuciya don kula da Maida Hankali game da sihiri da jefa sihiri mataki ɗaya ne mafi sauƙi.
- **Babban Korar Undead (Greater Turning):** Yana buƙatar Ƙarfin Zuciya na 60% ko fiye. Ana iya sake jefa Ikon Korar Undead idan ana so, duk da haka dole ne a yi amfani da sabon jefa.
- **Dukan Tsarki (Holy Smite):** Yana buƙatar Ibada da Ƙarfin Zuciya na 50% ko fiye. Limami na iya ƙara Gyaran Lalacewar su (Damage Modifier) da mataki ɗaya akan waɗanda suka mutu, aljanu, da shedanu. Ana iya amfani da shi sau ɗaya a rana a kowane Matsayi da aka kai.
- **Ƙarfin Tunani I (Mental Strength I):** Yana buƙatar Ibada na 60% ko fiye. Sau ɗaya a rana za ku ga ya fi sauƙi da matakai 2 don yin binciken Ƙarfin Zuciya lokacin da kuke tsayayya da kowane nau'in sihiri da ke shafar tunani. Wannan ya haɗa da iko kamar lalata, fara'a, rinjaye, riƙewa, hypnosis, Magic Jar, mallaka, barci, shawara, da psionic mind blast. Ba shi da wani tasiri akan basira kamar Tasiri ko Seduction. Haka kuma za ku ga ya fi sauƙi da mataki 1 don rashin yarda da rudu da tsammani. A yanayin elves, wannan ba ya haɗuwa da juriyarsu ga Barci da Fara'a, amma yana faɗaɗa ikonsu na tsayayya da ƙarin sakamako.
- **Daidaito na Makami (Weapon Precision):** Kuna da gwaninta wajen amfani da ƙananan makamai, waɗanda ba sa buƙatar ƙarfi da yawa, a ɓoye da inganci. Wannan yana ba ku damar lissafin Gyaran Lalacewar da waɗannan makamai bisa ga STR+DEX maimakon STR+SIZ idan zai haifar da kyakkyawan sakamako. Gyaran Lalacewar ba zai iya wuce lalacewar makami ta al'ada ba. Misali, wuka mai lalacewar 1d4+1, za a iyakance ta zuwa Gyaran Lalacewar na +1d4, yana mai da ita 1d4+1+1d4. Wannan ya haɗa da kulob, wuka, garrotes, wukake, gajerun takubba, main gauche, da rapiers, da kuma hare-haren da ba a yi amfani da makami ba. Darts, slings, gajerun baka, da javelins kuma suna faɗuwa cikin wannan rukunin.

#### Matsayi 2:

Don kowane kashe 2 na Jijjiga Gwaninta za ka iya samun ɗaya daga cikin waɗannan Ikon (kowane ana iya ɗauka sau ɗaya kawai):

- **Ƙarin Halayyar (Characteristic Increase):** Kuna iya ƙara +1 zuwa Halayyar da kuka zaɓa. Wannan ba zai iya wuce iyakar launin fata ba.
- **Martanin Tsaro I (Defensive Reflexes I):** Martanin Tsaro yana buƙatar ku kasance marasa nauyi (ɗaukar 'Abubuwa' ƙasa da STR), sanye da abin da bai fi sulke na haske nauyi ba. Sau ɗaya a kowane yaƙi kuna iya sake jefa gazawar Parry ko Evade ba tare da kashe Maki na Sa’a ba.
- **Ƙarin Korar Undead (Extra Turning):** Yana buƙatar Ibada da Ƙarfin Zuciya na 80% ko fiye. Limami na iya ninka adadin lokutan da za su iya Korar Undead tsakanin hutawa. Sau biyu a Matsayi 1, sau huɗu a Matsayi 3, kuma sau shida a Matsayi 5.
- **Ƙarfin Tunani II (Mental Strength II):** Yana buƙatar Ƙarfin Tunani I da Ibada na 80% ko fiye. Kamar Ƙarfin Tunani I, amma ana iya amfani da shi sau biyu a rana.
- **Taɓawar Nesa (Ranged Touch) (X):** Yana buƙatar Ƙarfin Zuciya na 80% ko fiye. Wannan Ikon yana ba wa mai jefa sihiri damar ba da sihiri tare da Range na "Touch", Range na ƙafa 30 maimakon. Ana ɗaukar sihiri a matsayin Matsayi ɗaya mafi girma dangane da sa a ƙwaƙwalwa kuma ya kamata a lura da shi azaman nesa lokacin da aka sa a ƙwaƙwalwa. Misali, Warkar da Ƙananan Raunuka (Nesa) zai ƙidaya azaman sihiri na Matsayi 2.

#### Matsayi 3:

Don kowane kashe 3 na Jijjiga Gwaninta za ka iya samun ɗaya daga cikin waɗannan Ikon (kowane ana iya ɗauka sau ɗaya kawai):

- **Ƙarin Halayyar (Characteristic Increase):** Kuna iya ƙara +1 zuwa Halayyar da kuka zaɓa. Wannan ba zai iya wuce iyakar launin fata ba.
- **Ingantaccen Dukan Tsarki (Improved Holy Smite):** Yana buƙatar Dukan Tsarki, da kuma Ibada da Ƙarfin Zuciya na 90% ko fiye. Gyaran Lalacewar Dukan Tsarki yanzu yana ƙaruwa da matakai biyu akan waɗanda suka mutu, aljanu, da shedanu.
- **Ƙarfin Tunani III (Mental Strength III):** Yana buƙatar Ƙarfin Tunani II da Ibada na 100% ko fiye. Kamar Ƙarfin Tunani I, amma ana iya amfani da shi sau uku a rana.

#### Matsayi 4:

Don kowane kashe 4 na Jijjiga Gwaninta za ka iya samun ɗaya daga cikin waɗannan Ikon (kowane ana iya ɗauka sau ɗaya kawai):

- **Ƙarin Halayyar (Characteristic Increase):** Kuna iya ƙara +1 zuwa Halayyar da kuka zaɓa. Wannan ba zai iya wuce iyakar launin fata ba.
- **Martanin Tsaro II (Defensive Reflexes II):** Yana buƙatar Martanin Tsaro I. Kamar Martanin Tsaro, duk da haka yanzu za ku iya amfani da wannan Ikon sau biyu a cikin yaƙi ɗaya. Duk da haka, ba za ku iya sake jefa gaba da hari ɗaya fiye da sau ɗaya ba.
- **Ƙarfin Tunani IV (Mental Strength IV):** Yana buƙatar Ƙarfin Tunani III da Rantsuwa ko Ibada na 120% ko fiye. Kamar Ƙarfin Tunani I, amma ana iya amfani da shi sau huɗu a rana.#### Mataki 5:

Don kowane kashewar 5 Experience Rolls, za ka iya samun ɗaya daga cikin waɗannan Ikon (kowane ɗaya ana iya ɗaukar shi sau ɗaya kacal):

- **Ƙara Hali (Characteristic Increase):** Za ka iya ƙara +1 ga Hali (Characteristic) da kake so. Wannan ba zai iya wuce iyakar kabilarka ba.
- **Babban Dukar Mai Tsarki (Greater Holy Smite):** Yana buƙatar Improved Holy Smite, tare da Bauta (Devotion) da Ƙarfin Zuciya (Willpower) na 130% ko fiye. Yanzu, Holy Smite's Damage Modifier ya ƙaru da matakai huɗu ga undead, aljanu, da shedanu!
- **Ƙarfin Hankali V (Mental Strength V):** Yana buƙatar Mental Strength IV da Rantsuwa (Oath) ko Bauta (Devotion) na 140% ko fiye. Kamar Mental Strength I, amma ana iya amfani da shi sau biyar a kowace rana.

### Juya Undead (Turning Undead)

Firistoci na kowane Alignment za su iya yunƙurin Juya kowane undead a cikin ƙafa 30. Firistoci na Mugunta (Evil clerics) na iya zaɓar su sarrafa undead maimakon hakan, idan suna so. Za ka iya yunƙurin Juya Undead sau ɗaya a Mataki na 1, sau biyu a Mataki na 3, kuma sau uku a Mataki na 5. Za ka sake samun duk amfani da Turn bayan ɗan hutun (Short Rest) na akalla awa 1.

_Misali, Alexandra ita ce firist ta Mataki 4. Tana iya yunƙurin juyawa sau biyu a lokacin faɗa, kuma idan ta huta akalla awa ɗaya bayan haka, za ta sake samun amfani duka biyun._

**Ƙarfin Juyawa (Turning Power):** Ƙarfin Juyawa na firist yana gyara jimlar Ƙarfin (Intensity) na undead waɗanda aka yi nasarar Juyawa. Wannan daidai yake da 1d6+2, +2 ga kowane ƙarin Mataki bayan 1, kuma ya kamata a lura da shi a kan Takardar Hali (Character Sheet), yayin da hali yake amfani da shi duk lokacin da ya yi nasara a yunƙurin Juya.

_Misali, a Mataki na 2, Alexandra tana da Ƙarfin Juyawa na 1d6+4_

Don yunƙurin Juyawa yana buƙatar gwajin fasaha (Opposed skill test) ta amfani da fasahar Channel vs. halittar da ta dace wacce ke da mafi girman Ƙarfin Zuciya (Willpower) a cikin Yankin Tasiri (Area of Effect). Digirin nasara yana ƙayyade tasirin yunƙurin.

Firistoci na Mugunta na iya ba da umarni ga Ƙarfin Juyawa a cikin Ƙarfin undead. Wannan tasirin yana ba wa firist damar ba da umarni ga undead amma ba ya sa su zama masu biyayya sai dai idan sun mirgina nasara mai mahimmanci (Critical Success) kuma suka zaɓi Enslave - duba ƙasa. Wadanda suke da mafi ƙarancin Ƙarfi (Intensity) su ne koyaushe ake shafar su da farko. Wannan ba shi da tasiri a kan undead masu Ƙarfi 7 ko fiye. Firist na mugunta na iya amfani da wannan don kawo undead marasa hankali da aka riga aka juya a ƙarƙashin ikonsu kuma.

- **Nasarar Mahimmanci (Critical Success):** Undead masu Ƙarfi (Intensity) sau biyu na Ƙarfin Juyawarka abin ya shafa. Idan Matakinka ya ninka Ƙarfin (Intensity) na halittar undead mara hankali sau biyu (ko fiye), za ka halakar da duk wanda kake son Juyawa kullum.
  - Firistoci na mugunta na iya maimakon haka ba da umarni ga undead masu Ƙarfi sau biyu na Ƙarfin Juyawarsu na tsawon Rounds 12 (minti 1). Tunda undead yawanci suna da tunani iri ɗaya da firist na mugunta, wannan tasirin yana ba wa hali damar ba da umarni ga undead. Ba ya sa su zama masu biyayya ba, kamar yadda yake tare da Enslave a ƙasa, kuma undead masu hankali ba za su kawar da rayuwarsu ba tare da dalili ba. Wadanda suke da mafi ƙarancin Ƙarfi (Intensity) su ne koyaushe ake shafar su da farko. Wannan ba shi da tasiri a kan undead masu Ƙarfi 7 ko fiye. Firist na mugunta na iya amfani da wannan don kawo undead marasa hankali da aka riga aka juya a ƙarƙashin iko kuma.
  - A madadin haka, firist na mugunta da ya sami Nasarar Mahimmanci na iya bautar da (Enslave) undead masu Ƙarfi daidai da Ƙarfin Juyawarsu, yana bautar da su ga ikonsu. Undead da aka bautar da su suna bin firist gaba ɗaya har sai an fasa ikonsu ta wata hanya. Wadanda suke da mafi ƙarancin Ƙarfi (Intensity) su ne koyaushe ake bautar da su da farko. Wannan ba shi da tasiri a kan undead masu Ƙarfi 7 ko fiye. Firist na mugunta na iya amfani da wannan don kawo undead da aka riga aka juya a ƙarƙashin iko kuma.
- **Nasarar (Success):** Undead masu Ƙarfi daidai da Ƙarfin Juyawarka abin ya shafa, farawa da waɗanda suke da mafi ƙarancin Ƙarfi (Intensity) da farko. Idan ba a mirgina isasshen Ƙarfin Juyawa don shafar komai ba, to babu abin da aka juya yayin wannan yunƙurin. Firist ɗin yana kawar da waɗanda aka shafa, waɗanda dole ne su motsa kuma su zauna aƙalla ƙafa 20 nesa na tsawon Rounds 12 (minti 1). Wadanda aka shafa ba za su iya kai hari ga kowane memba na ƙungiyar firist ɗin da suka rage a cikin Yankin Tasiri (Area of Effect) ba sai idan an tilasta waɗanda aka shafa su shiga cikin ƙafa 10 na ƙungiyar. Wadanda ba za su iya motsawa cikakken nisa ba saboda filin da ke tsakanin su suna tsorata (duk wani mirgina na Hari (Attack rolls) a kansu yana da sauƙi da Digiri ɗaya na Wahala). Kusantowa cikin ƙafa 10 na waɗanda aka shafa yana sa undead su shawo kan kasancewa Juyayyu kuma su yi aiki yadda ya kamata. Hare-haren nesa ba kusa da ƙafa 10 ba ba sa fasa tasirin Juyawa; duk da haka, irin waɗannan hare-haren a kan undead mai hankali zai fasa tasirin Juyawa, yana ba da damar halittar ta amsa yadda ya kamata. Halittu masu hankali, da zarar an Juya su, ana ba su izinin mirgina Ƙarfin Zuciya (Willpower) mai wuya a kowane Round don ƙoƙarin fasa tasirin; duk da haka, halittu masu hankali mai yiwuwa za su gudu daga yankin da son rai kuma su dawo kawai lokacin da suke da fa'ida kuma. Idan Matakin firist ɗin ya ninka Ƙarfin (Intensity) na halittar undead mara hankali sau biyu (ko fiye), firist ɗin yana halakar da duk wanda zai yi Juyawa kullum.
  - Firist na mugunta na iya ba da umarni ga halittun undead masu Ƙarfi daidai da Ƙarfin Juyawarsu, yana kawo su a ƙarƙashin ikonsu. Wannan tasirin yana ba wa firist damar ba da umarni ga undead. Ba ya sa su zama masu biyayya ba, kamar yadda yake tare da Nasarar Mahimmanci, kuma undead masu hankali ba za su kawar da rayuwarsu ba tare da dalili ba. Wadanda suke da mafi ƙarancin Ƙarfi (Intensity) su ne koyaushe ake shafar su da farko. Wannan ba shi da tasiri a kan undead masu Ƙarfi 7 ko fiye. Firist na mugunta na iya amfani da wannan don kawo undead marasa hankali da aka riga aka juya a ƙarƙashin ikonsu kuma.
- **Rashin Nasara (Failure):** Babu undead da aka Juya.
- **Kuskure (Fumble):** Ba kawai ka kasa Juya komai ba, amma ka ja hankalin undead da yawa daidai da sau biyu na Ƙarfin Juyawarka a cikin Ƙarfi (Intensity). Wadannan halittu suna kai hari a kanka musamman, tare da halittu masu mafi ƙarancin Ƙarfi (Intensity) waɗanda aka tsokane su da farko.

## Jarumi (Fighter)

Jarumi ƙwararrun sojoji ne, sojojin haya, da mayaƙa, kuma su ne mafi yawan masu neman kasada a duniyoyin tatsuniyoyi. Ko suna rushe ƙofofi, suna amfani da takubba da gatari tare da ƙwarewa da fasaha, ko kuma suna tsaka tsakiyar mambobin ƙungiyar da ba su da ƙwarewar yaƙi da abokan gaba, duk inda kuka sami kasada, za ku sami jarumi.

#### Jinsi/Al'ada gama gari

Kowane.

#### Hali (Characteristic) Mai Mahimmanci

STR, CON, da DEX

#### Alignment/Bukatun Rantsuwa

- Alignment: Jarumai ba su da iyakancewa ta hanyar Alignment kuma ba sa buƙatar Rantsuwa.

Duba [Babi na 4: Alignment da So](0004_Alignment_and_Passions.md).

#### Fasahar Aji (Class Skills)

- **Fasahar Daidaitacce:** Athletics, Boating ko Ride, Brawn, Combat Skill (Fighter)\*, Endurance, Evade, Unarmed\*
- **Fasahar Ƙwararru:** Craft. (kowane), Engineering, Gambling, Intimidation, Lore (Military History), Lore (Strategy and Tactics), Oratory, Survival ko Navigate

_\* Yana karɓar +5 na kari daga Combat Proficiency_

#### Sulke da Ƙwarewar Makami na Jarumi

Jarumai sun ƙware da kowane nau'in sulke (mara nauyi da mai nauyi), kuma da kowane garkuwa. Fasaharsu ta yaƙi (Combat Skill) tana haɗawa da amfani da duk makaman yau da kullun da na yaƙi.

Lokacin da aka kai wannan matakin cancanta, ana samun ƙarin Luck Point, kuma ana iya samun sabbin Ikon. #### Ikon Farko (Starting Abilities)

Jarumi ya san duk waɗannan Ikon a Mataki na 1. Bugu da ƙari, yayin ƙirƙirar hali, za ka iya zaɓar Ikon Mataki na 1 ɗaya kyauta idan ka cika buƙatunsa.

- **Combat Proficiency:** Kasancewa ƙwararre a yaƙi da makami, jarumai suna samun ƙarin +5% zuwa Combat Skill da Unarmed skill.
- **Weapon Specialization:** Horon yaƙinka ya ba ka damar zaɓar makami guda ɗaya don ƙwarewa ta musamman, wannan na iya zama garkuwa kuma. Weapon specialization yana ba da fa'idodi dangane da takamaiman makamin da aka zaɓa kuma an taƙaita shi a ƙasa.
  - **Makamin Yaƙin Kusa (Melee Weapon):** Kuna karɓar fa'idojin ƙwarewa masu zuwa zuwa makamin yaƙin kusa guda ɗaya: 
    - Combat Skill ɗinka yana inganta da +5% lokacin amfani da makamin ƙwarewarka. Wannan kari ba zai taimaka a ci gaban Mataki ba.  
    - Kuna samun kari na Parry lokacin riƙe wannan makamin. Wannan ba ya buƙatar amfani da Action Point.
  - **Makamin Harbin Nesa (Ranged Weapon):** Kuna karɓar fa'idojin ƙwarewa masu zuwa zuwa Makamin Harbin Nesa guda ɗaya:  
    - Idan ba a ba ka mamaki ba, za ka iya harbi ta atomatik da farko (kafin mirgina don Initiative) a farkon yaƙi, idan duka makami da ammo an shirya su. Kuna bayyana niyyarku ta harbi, warware sakamakon, sannan Initiative ya mirgina.  
    - Lokacin da kake nufin makasudi a cikin Close Range na makamin, nufin yana buƙatar Turn 1 na daidaita makamin maimakon cikakken Round, kuma yana sa harin da ke biyo baya ya zama digiri 1 cikin sauƙi. Hare-haren da aka yi fiye da Close Range suna bin ƙa'idodin yau da kullun don nufin kamar yadda aka gani ƙarƙashin [Ranged Combat](0008_Combat.md?id=ranged-combat) a Babi na 8. Ƙarin Rounds na nufin ba sa ba da ƙarin fa'ida.  
    - Ƙwararre na iya rage lokacin sake lodi (reload time) na kowane Makamin Harbin Nesa da aka ƙware da 1. Makami mai lokacin sake lodi 0 na iya zama a shirya azaman Free Action.
  - **Garkuwa (Shields):** Kuna karɓar fa'idojin ƙwarewa masu zuwa zuwa DUK garkuwoyi:  
    - Combat Skill ɗinka lokacin amfani da kowane garkuwa yana inganta da +10%. Wannan ƙarin fasaha ba zai taimaka a ci gaban Mataki ba.  
    - Duk garkuwoyi suna ƙidaya azaman nau'in Girman (Size) ɗaya mafi girma don dalilai na lalacewar Parry: Large zuwa Huge, Huge zuwa Enormous, da Enormous zuwa Colossal.

#### Ikon Mataki (Ranked Abilities)

Kamar yadda sunan ke nunawa, Ikon Mataki ana iya zaɓar su ne kawai ta hanyar hali na wani Mataki ko fiye. Yawancinsu suna da Fasaha ta Bukatu (Prerequisite Skill) wacce dole ne ta kasance a wani matakin fasaha ko fiye. Ba a hana ku shiga Ikon da suka gabata lokacin da aka kai sabon Mataki ba. Idan kun kasa ɗaukar Ikon Mataki na 2 alal misali, za ku iya komawa ku ɗauke shi a Mataki na 3 ko ma bayan haka. Ba za a iya ɗaukar fiye da Ikon ɗaya kowace zama ba.

#### Mataki 1:

Don kowane kashewar 1 Experience Roll, za ka iya samun ɗaya daga cikin waɗannan Ikon (Ikon ɗaya kowace zama, kowane ɗaya ana iya ɗaukar shi sau ɗaya kacal):

- **Forceful Strike:** Yana buƙatar Brawn da Combat Skill na 50% ko fiye. Ta hanyar rage fasaharka da digiri ɗaya a harin yaƙin kusa na gaba, za ka iya ƙara Damage Modifier ɗinka da digiri biyu.
- **Just a Scratch:** Yana buƙatar Endurance na 50% ko fiye. Sau ɗaya a kowace rana, bayan ɗaukar ɗan gajeren hutu na akalla awa ɗaya da duba raunukanka, za ka iya sake samun adadin Hit Points a wani Hit Location daidai da Healing Rate ɗinka. Ba shi da tasiri a kan Major Wounds.
- **Mounted Combat:** Yana buƙatar Ride Skill na 50% ko fiye. Ka horar da kanka don yaƙi daga bayan dabba mai hau (mount) kuma za ka iya amfana daga abubuwan da ke biyo baya lokacin da kake kan dabba. Inda yawanci fasahar yaƙi na masu hau ba za su iya wuce fasaharsu ta Ride ba, wannan dokar ba ta shafeka ba. Bugu da ƙari, yin mounted charge ba ya jawo hukuncin wahala mataki ɗaya na buga wa (to hit). A ƙarshe, za ka iya yin watsi da digiri na hukunci na farko na kowane mirgina Ride da ake buƙata yayin yaƙi. Lura cewa makiyaya ƴan adam da masu hau elven na iya amfani da kari na sama lokacin amfani da baka gajere (short bow) daga bayan dabba mai hau.
- **Second Wind:** Yana buƙatar Endurance na 50% ko fiye. Sau ɗaya a kowace rana, bayan samun damar ɗaukar ɗan gajeren hutu na akalla awa ɗaya, za ka iya dawo da har zuwa matakai 3 na gajiyar (Fatigue) da aka rasa nan take sama da duk abin da aka samu yadda ya kamata.
- **Skirmishing:** Yana buƙatar Athletics da Combat Skill na 50% ko fiye. Wannan Ikon yana ba da damar ƙaddamar da harin nesa yayin zaɓar Hustle Action don gudu (amma ba yayin sprinting ba). Duk da haka, kashi na harin mai kai harin ba zai iya wuce fasaharsa ta Athletics ba. Harin na iya faruwa kafin ko bayan motsi.
- **Weapon Precision:** Kai ƙwararre ne wajen amfani da ƙananan makamai waɗanda ba sa buƙatar ƙarfi da yawa, a ɓoye da kuma yadda ya kamata. Wannan yana ba ka damar ƙididdige Damage Modifier tare da waɗannan makaman bisa STR+DEX maimakon STR+SIZ idan zai samar da sakamako mafi kyau. Damage Modifier ba zai iya wuce kashi na lalacewar makami na yau da kullun ba (normal damage die). Misali, wuka tare da lalacewar 1d4+1, za a iyakance ta da Damage Modifier na +1d4, yana sa ta zama 1d4+1+1d4. Wannan ya haɗa da kulob, wuka, garrote, wuƙaƙe, takubba gajeru, main gauche, da rapier. haka kuma hare-hare marasa makami. Darts, slings, short bows, da javelins su ma suna cikin wannan nau'in.
- **Weapon Specialization:** Yana buƙatar Combat Skill na 50% ko fiye. Za ka iya zaɓar wani Weapon Specialization.

#### Mataki 2:

Bayan kai Mataki na 2, za ka ci gaba ta atomatik zuwa Weapon Master idan ka cika buƙatunsa.

- **Weapon Master** (takamaiman makamin yaƙin kusa ko Harbin Nesa): Yana buƙatar Weapon Specialization a makamin da aka zaɓa da Combat Skill na 70% ko fiye. Yayin da za ka iya ƙware a kowane adadin makamai, za ka iya zama babban ƙwararre (master) a ɗaya kacal. Saboda haka, ba za ka iya ɗaukar wannan Ikon ba idan ka riga ka ɗauki weapon mastery a kowane makami. Weapon masters suna ninka damar Critical na makamansu da suka ƙware. Bugu da ƙari, makamin yaƙin kusa da aka zaɓa yana samun ingantawa mataki 1 zuwa Damage Modifier yayin da Makamin Harbin Nesa ke samun kari iri ɗaya a kan makasudi a cikin Close Range.

Bugu da ƙari, don kowane kashewar 2 Experience Rolls, za ka iya samun ɗaya daga cikin waɗannan Ikon (kowane ɗaya ana iya ɗaukar shi sau ɗaya kacal):

- **Brute Strength:** Sau ɗaya a kowace rana fasaharka ta Brawn tana zama digiri ɗaya cikin sauƙi don yin Action ɗaya. Kana shan wahalar asarar mataki ɗaya na Gajiyar (Fatigue) bayan hakan.
- **Characteristic Increase:** Za ka iya ƙara +1 ga Hali (Characteristic) da kake so. Wannan ba zai iya wuce iyakar kabilarka ba.
- **Sweeping Strike:** Yana buƙatar Forceful Strike da Combat Skill na 70% ko fiye. Sweeping Strike ana yin sa ta hanyar amfani da mirgina harin yaƙin kusa guda ɗaya zuwa makasudi biyu a cikin iska da kuma a cikin layin gani (line of sight) naka. Hare-haren ba za su iya wuce ta wani aboki da aka sanya tsakanin abokan gaba biyu ba. Sweeping Strike ɗaya kawai za a iya yi a kowane Round.
- **Weapon Specialization:** Yana buƙatar Combat Skill na 70% ko fiye. Za ka iya zaɓar wani Weapon Specialization.#### Matsayi na 3:

##  Mai Amfani da Sihiri (Mage)

Masu amfani da sihiri (Magic-users) suna nazarin abubuwan da ba a sani ba, suna haƙa cikin tsofaffin kaburbura masu ƙura don nemo sabbin sihiri masu wuya don ƙarawa a littattafansu na sihiri. Suna kashe mafi yawan lokutansu na hutu suna nazarin waɗannan sihiri kuma suna koyon yadda za su mallaki ikon da ke cikinsu. Ko da yake suna da rauni a farko, masu sihiri suna da yuwuwar zama mafi ƙarfi a cikin Ajujuwa (Classes).

#### Jinsi/Al'ada gama gari

Duk wani, amma yawanci mutanen da suka ci gaba (Civilized) da kuma na ƙaura (Nomadic), gami da gnomes, elves, da half-elves. Gnomes da suka zama masu sihiri sukan karkata zuwa ga Makarantar Ruɗi (School of Illusion).

#### Muhimman Halaye

DEX, INT, da POW

#### Sharuɗɗan Daidaitawa/Rantsuwa

- Daidaitawa (Alignment): Masu sihiri ba su da iyaka ta fuskar Daidaitawa kuma ba sa buƙatar Rantsuwa.

Duba [Babi na 4: Daidaitawa da Sha'awa](0004_Alignment_and_Passions.md).

#### Kwarewar Aji (Class Skills)

- **Kwarewa ta Musamman (Standard Skills):** Kaucewa (Evade), Taimakon Farko (First Aid), Tasiri (Influence), Fahimta (Insight), Wuri (Locale), Hange (Perception), Ƙarfin Zuciya (Willpower)
- **Kwarewar Ƙwararru (Professional Skills):** Mai sihiri yana samun Jefa Sihiri na Asiri (Arcane Casting) da Ilimin Asiri (Arcane Knowledge) ta atomatik, tare da ƙarin ƙwarewa 3 daga waɗannan: Al'ada (kowanne), Harshe (kowanne), Ilimin Alchimiya (Lore - Alchemy), Ilimi (kowanne)

#### Kwarewa a Kayan Yaƙi da Makami na Mai Sihiri

Masu sihiri ba su da ƙwarewa da kowane nau'in kayan yaƙi ko garkuwa, domin sihiri na Asiri (Arcane spells) yana buƙatar motsi daidai yayin jefawa, wanda sanya kayan yaƙi yake hana shi. Yawanci ba a horar da su a yaƙin jiki ba; don haka, ƙwarewar yaƙi na mai sihiri za a iya haɓaka shi ne kawai da Mambobin Ƙwarewa na Ƙari (Bonus Skill Points). Ya haɗa da amfani da wukake, kibiyoyi, jifa, sanda, da ƙananan bakuna na ƙetare (light crossbows), makamai masu sauƙi da aka saba samu a kusa da mazaunin malamin su ko kuma waɗanda suke da sauƙin ɗauka da koyo.

#### Ikon Farko

- **Mai Jefa Sihiri (Spellcaster):** Duk da cewa masu sihiri suna da rauni sosai a farkon ayyukansu, a ɗaya gefen, za su iya jefa sihiri mafi ban sha'awa da ƙarfi, suna iya ƙone sojoji, kuma su ruguza gidajen sarauta ba tare da wani hukunci ba. #### Ikon Matsayi (Ranked Abilities)

Ba a hana ka shiga tsofaffin Ikon ba lokacin da aka kai sabon Matsayi. Idan ka kasa ɗaukar Ikon Rank 2 alal misali, za ka iya komawa ka ɗauke shi a Rank 3 ko ma bayan haka. Ba za a ɗauki fiye da Ikon ɗaya a kowane zama ba.

Sau ɗaya a rana, za ka ga cewa yana da sauƙi da mataki 2 na Wahala a yi jarabawar Ƙarfin zuciya yayin tsayayya da kowane nau'in sihiri da ke nufin zuciya. Wannan ya haɗa da iko kamar beguiling, charm, domination, hold, hypnosis, Magic Jar, possession, sleep, suggestion, da psionic mind blast. Ba shi da tasiri a kan ƙwarewa kamar Tasiri (Influence) ko Seduction. Haka kuma za ka ga yana da sauƙi da mataki ɗaya don ƙin yarda (Disbelieve) da ruɗi da hasashe. Wannan ya haɗa da sanduna, wukake, garrotes, knives, shortswords, main gauche, da rapiers. Har ma da hare-haren hannu. Darts, slings, short bows, da javelins su ma suna cikin wannan rukuni.

## Rogue (Ɗan Fashi/Mai Sata)

Rogue ƙwararru ne a ɓoye-ɓoye, waɗanda suka ƙware a ɓoyayyen tafiya, tarkuna, buɗe makulli, da sauran ƙwarewar da al'umma sukan kalla da zato. Duk da haka, yayin da dukan ɓarayin (thieves) suke rogues, ba kowane rogue ne ɓarawo ba. Rogues suna rayuwa ta hanyar basirarsu, suna amfani da ƙwarewarsu na shiga inda aka haramta da ɓoyayyen tafiya don alherin kowa. Duk da haka, alherin kowa sau da yawa ya dogara ne akan wane gefen lu'u-lu'u kake tsaye.

#### Nau'in/Al'ada gama gari

Duk wanda yake

#### Halaye Masu Mahimmanci

DEX, INT, da CHA

#### Bukatun Daidaituwa/Rantsuwa

- Daidaituwa (Alignment): Rogues ba a iyakance su da Daidaituwa ba kuma ba sa buƙatar Rantsuwa sai dai idan membobin ƙungiyar ɓarayin (thieves' guild) ne.
- Rantsuwar Ƙungiyar Ɓarayi 30% da ƙari da INT+POW na hali.

Duba [Babi na 4: Daidaituwa da Sha'awa](0004_Alignment_and_Passions.md).

Baya ga kowane Daidaituwa da Sha'awa na kabilanci/al'adu, rogues waɗanda suka zaɓi shiga ƙungiyar ɓarayin dole ne su rantse kuma su bi duk ƙa'idodin da aka shimfiɗa a [Amfani da Daidaituwa da Sha'awa](0004_Alignment_and_Passions.md?id=using-alignment-and-passions). Bugu da ƙari, dole ne su bi waɗannan tsauraran ƙa'idodi: nuna aminci ga sauran membobi, bi duk ƙa'idodin ƙungiyar, yin abin da yake a mafi kyawun sha'awar ƙungiyar (sanya ƙungiyar kafin sha'awar kashin kai), kiyaye asirin ƙungiyar, biyan kuɗin ƙungiyar, da kuma ba da wani ɓangare na kowane aiki da ƙungiyar ta 'amince' da shi.

Kuɗin ƙungiyar ya bambanta gwargwadon wuri da girman ƙungiyar, amma kusan zinariya 100 a kowace shekara ita ce misali. Baya ga kuɗin shekara, ana buƙatar membobi su ba da wani ɓangare na kowane aiki inda ƙungiyar ta taimaka ta hanyar lambobin sadarwa, bayanin sirri, taswirori, bayanin gabaɗaya, da sauransu. Ƙayyadadden adadin ɓangaren ya kamata a ƙaddara shi bisa ga kowane yanayi, ya danganta da taimakon da aka bayar, amma matsakaicin 20%, wanda aka gyara sama ko ƙasa yana da kyau. A musaya, ƙungiyar tana ba da matsuguni na ɗan lokaci ga memba da kawayensa, da kuma duk sauran ayyukan da Games Master ya ga sun dace. Akwai masu karɓar kayan sata (fences) don taimakawa wajen sayar da kayan da aka sace, kuma ta hanyar waɗannan lambobin sadarwa, ana sa ran ɓarawo zai sami kusan ¼ na ƙimar sayar da abin. Yawancin ƙungiyoyin ɓarayi suna da kasancewa a mafi yawan manyan birane har ma da wasu manyan garuruwa.

Rogue na iya zaɓar shiga ƙungiyar ɓarayi bayan ƙirƙirar hali idan Games Master ya yarda. Yayin da shiga ƙungiyar ɓarayi lokacin ƙirƙirar hali ba ya kashe hali komai, shiga daga baya yana kashe Zinariya 100 kowane Rank na yanzu, kuma yana ɗaukar wata 1 na horo da shiga. Bayan lokacin da aka ware, halin yana ɗaukar taken ƙungiyar ɓarayi da ya dace da Rank ɗinsa kuma yana ƙara Rantsuwar Ƙungiyar Ɓarayi a 30% da ƙari da POW+INT.

#### Ƙwarewa (Skills)

Saboda yanayinsu daban-daban, rogues suna da ƙarin zaɓin ƙwarewa fiye da adadin da aka saba. Duk da haka, ba sa karɓar ƙarin Maki na Ƙwarewa (Skill Points).

- **Ƙwarewar Da aka Saba:** Athletics, Ƙwarewar Yaƙi (Rogue), Ɓatarwa (Deceit), Gujewa (Evade), Fahimta (Insight), Gani (Perception), Ɓoyayyen tafiya (Stealth)
- **Ƙwarewar Ƙwararru:** Rogue ta atomatik yana karɓar Buɗe Makulli (Lockpicking) da Makanikai (Mechanisms), da ƙarin ƙwarewa biyu daga waɗannan: Acrobatics, Acting, Kasuwanci (Commerce), Ɓoye Siffa (Disguise), Tsoratarwa (Intimidation), Jaraba (Seduction), Zamba (Sleight), ko Wayewar titi (Streetwise). A lokacin ƙirƙirar hali, membobin Ƙungiyar Ɓarayin kuma suna karɓar Harshe (Yaren Ɓarayi/Thieves' Cant) a +40. Idan suka ɗauke shi daga baya, ba sa karɓar ƙarin bonus na +40.

#### Rogues da Makamai

Rogues suna ƙware da kowane nau'in makamai (makauma da nauyi), da kowane garkuwa (ban da garkuwar hasumiya). Duk da haka, suna fuskantar takunkumi akan yawancin Ikon rogue lokacin da suke sanye da sulke mai nauyi ko lokacin da suke da nauyi. Ƙwarewarsu ta Yaƙi ta haɗa da amfani da duk makamai na asali, da kuma ƙananan bakan hannu (hand crossbows), dogayen takuba (longswords), rapiers, da gajerun takuba (shortswords).

### Tsarin Rank

Dukan rogues ana ƙididdige su ta Rank, wanda shi ne ƙimar ƙarfi da ƙwarewa. Kuna iya zaɓar ko dai taken rogue ko ɓarawo, ya danganta da wanne ne ya fi dacewa da alaƙarku da ƙungiyar ɓarayi. Lokacin da aka sami ƙayyadadden matakin ƙwarewa a cikin ƙananan ƙwarewar Class, rogues na iya haɓaka Rank ɗinsu, kamar yadda za su nuna matakin da ya dace na kwarewa. Lokacin da aka kai wannan matakin na kwarewa, ana samun ƙarin Luck Point kuma ana iya samun sabbin Iko. Ana samun wannan bayanin akan tebur mai zuwa ƙarƙashin Advancement. Da ɗaukar cewa ka cika abubuwan da ake buƙata, halinka yana farawa azaman Rank 1 Scoundrel ko Burglar tare da Ikon da aka lura a ƙasa; in ba haka ba, ka fara azaman Rank 0 Scamp ko Cutpurse ba tare da wasu Iko ba.

#### Ikon Farko

Rogue ya san duk waɗannan Ikon a Rank 1. Bugu da ƙari, yayin ƙirƙirar hali, zaka iya zaɓar Ikon Rank 1 guda ɗaya kyauta da ɗaukar cewa ka cika buƙatunsa.

- **Hawan Ganuwa (Climb Walls):** Kana da ƙwarewa musamman wajen hawan komai har zuwa saman ganuwa. Idan ba ka da kaya (Less 'Things' carried than STR), kuma ba ka sanye da komai fiye da sulke mai sauƙi, kana iya watsi da Matsayin Wahala 1 (Difficulty Grade) zuwa jefa ƙuri'arka na Athletics lokacin yin kowane ƙoƙarin hawa. Bugu da ƙari, Takunkumin Sulke (Armor Penalty) akan Matsakaicin Motsi (Base Movement) lokacin hawa ana iya watsi da shi.
- **Ɓuya a Inuwa (Hide in Shadows):** Kai ƙwararre ne a ɓoyayyen tafiya kuma ka kammala fasahar ɓuya a inuwa. Idan za ka iya sanya kanka a wani yanki da inuwa ta rufe, duk jefa ƙuri'ar Gani (Perception) don ganinka suna da Matsayin Wahala 1 (Difficulty Grade) mafi wahala.
- **Harinsa na Ɓoye (Sneak Attack):** Rogue na iya ƙoƙarin tsayar da wanda aka azabtar daga matsayin da ya dace idan yana ɗauke da ƙaramin ko matsakaicin makami na yaƙi ko amfani da Makami na Nesa (Ranged Weapon) a cikin ƙafa 30 daga wanda aka azabtar. Dole ne ainihin wanda aka hari ya zama ɗan adam kuma yana da gabobin jiki masu mahimmanci. Ba shi da tasiri akan dodo ko taro na gelatinous, alal misali. Saboda maharin yawanci yana iya bugawa ba tare da cikas ba, suna samun Zaɓin Wuri (Choose Location) azaman bonus na Special Effect idan aka yi nasara. Duk da haka, dole ne wurin da aka zaɓa ya zama kan, ƙirji, ko ciki na wanda aka hari don samun kowane daga cikin waɗannan ƙarin fa'idodin.
  - Maharin na iya watsi da duk wani mara kyau na Damage Modifier wanda yawanci yake aiki, Special Effects Bypass Armor da Maximize Damage ba sa buƙatar Nasara ta Critical.
  - Ba lallai ba ne ga rogue ya sami mamaki akan wanda aka azabtar; duk da haka, dole ne a yi harin daga wajen filin gani na wanda aka azabtar. Saboda haka, yawancin rogues suna jira har sai abokin hamayya ya shiga faɗa da wani aboki sannan su yi amfani da hakan don bugawa daga baya. Parrying ko Evading wani Sneak Attack da farko yana buƙatar jefa ƙuri'ar Perception, Hard Perception jefa ƙuri'a idan wanda aka azabtar ya riga ya shagala. Idan aka yi nasara, kare kanka daga harin da ke zuwa daga gefe shine Formidable, yayin da kare kanka daga harin da ke zuwa daga baya shine aiki na Herculean! Sneak Attack ana iya amfani da shi akan wanda aka hari sau ɗaya kawai idan sun ci gaba da shagala ko kuma ba su san maharinsu ba.
- **Zamba (Subterfuge):** Saboda rogues su ne masters na shiga inda aka haramta, ƙwarewar Lockpicking, Mechanisms, da Sleight suna da Matsayin Wahala 1 mafi sauƙi.

#### Ikon Rank

Kamar yadda sunan ya nuna, Ikon Rank ana iya zaɓar su ne kawai ta hanyar hali na wani Rank ko sama da haka. Yawancin suna da Prerequisite Skill wanda dole ne ya zama a wani matakin ƙwarewa ko sama da haka. Ba a hana ka shiga Ikon Rank na baya lokacin da aka kai wani sabon Rank ba. Idan ka kasa ɗaukar Ikon Rank 2 alal misali, kana iya komawa ka ɗauke shi a Rank 3 ko ma daga baya. Ba za a iya ɗaukar fiye da Ikon Rank ɗaya a kowane zama ba.

#### Rank 1:

Don kowane kashe na Experience Roll guda 1 Rogue na iya samun ɗaya daga cikin waɗannan Ikon (kone ana iya ɗaukarsa sau ɗaya kawai):

- **Agile:** Yana buƙatar Evade ko Acrobatics a 60% ko sama da haka. Da ɗaukar cewa ba ka da kaya (Less 'Things' carried than STR) kuma ba ka sanye da komai fiye da sulke mai sauƙi, kana iya amfani da reflexes ɗinka na musamman a cikin yaƙi. Lokacin ƙididdige Initiative, kana iya ƙara +4 zuwa jimlar.
- **Artful Dodger:** Yana buƙatar Evade na 50% ko sama da haka. Wannan baiwa tana buƙatar ka zama ba ka da kaya (Less 'Things' carried than STR) kuma ba ka sanye da komai fiye da sulke mai sauƙi. Kana da reflexes masu sauri kuma kana iya amfani da ƙwarewar Evade don kauce wa harin yaƙi ba tare da faɗuwa ba. Akan harin nesa, kana faɗuwa ne kawai idan ka kasa jefa ƙuri'ar (Fail the roll).
- **Great Hearing:** Yana buƙatar Perception na 50% ko sama da haka. Jinka mai kaifi yana sa duk jefa ƙuri'ar Perception waɗanda suka shafi ji su zama Matsayin Wahala 1 mafi sauƙi.
- **Sharp Eyed:** Yana buƙatar Perception na 50% ko sama da haka. Ganinka mai kaifi yana sa duk jefa ƙuri'ar Perception waɗanda suka shafi gani su zama Matsayin Wahala 1 mafi sauƙi.
- **Skirmishing:** Yana buƙatar Athletics da Combat Skill na 50% ko sama da haka. Wannan Ikon yana ba da damar ƙaddamar da harin nesa a cikin Round guda wanda kake gudu (amma ba yayin da kake yin gudun sauri ba). Duk da haka, kashi na harin maharin ba zai iya wuce ƙwarewar Athletics ɗinsa ba.
- **Swashbuckling:** Yana buƙatar Combat Skill na 50% ko sama da haka. Da ɗaukar cewa halin ba shi da kaya (Less 'Things' carried than STR) kuma ba ya sanye da komai fiye da sulke mai sauƙi, za su iya shiga cikin hare-hare da gujewa da aka yi yayin tsalle ko juyawa cikin (ko fita daga) yaƙi, watsi da duk wani ƙimar ƙwarewa da ƙwarewar Athletics ta sanya akai.
- **Unarmored Defense:** Yana buƙatar Ikon Artful Dodger da ko dai Evade ko Acrobatics a 50% ko sama da haka. Halinka yana da matukar wahalar buguwa lokacin da sulke ba ya hana shi. Lokacin da ba ka da kaya (Less 'Things' carried than STR) kuma ba ka sanye da KO WANI sulke, jefa ƙuri'arka na Evade suna da Matsayin Wahala 1 mafi sauƙi.
- **Vaulting** (yana buƙatar Acrobatics): Yana buƙatar Acrobatics a 50% ko sama da haka. Lokacin da ba ka da kaya (Less 'Things' carried than STR), kana iya amfani da ƙwarewar Acrobatics ɗinka don yin tsalle da sanda bisa ga waɗannan ƙa'idodi. Yin amfani da sanda mai tsayin ƙaramin sanda aƙalla kuma motsawa aƙalla a gudun gudu ko gudun sauri, kana iya tsalle akan cikas ko sama zuwa wuraren da ba a iya kaiwa. Wannan yana buƙatar nasarar jefa ƙuri'ar Easy Acrobatics, kuma idan aka yi nasara, zai ba ka damar tsalle a kwance har zuwa sau biyu na tsayinka +1d4+6 ƙafa. Tsalle a tsaye mai nasara zai ba ka damar kai tsayi daidai da tsayin sandar (ƙafa 6 zuwa 9); duk da haka, yin tsalle a tsaye yana sa ka faɗi ƙasa. Hali ba tare da wannan Ikon ba wanda ke da ƙwarewar Acrobatics na iya ƙoƙarin yin wannan aikin a wahala ta yau da kullun. Akan nasara ta Critical tare da tsalle a kwance, ana iya ƙara nisan da aka yi tsalle da rabi. Nasara ta Critical yayin tsalle a tsaye tana ba ka damar sauka akan ƙafafunka.
- **Weapon Precision:** Kai ƙwararre ne wajen amfani da ƙananan makamai waɗanda za a iya ɓoyewa waɗanda ba sa buƙatar ƙarfi da yawa, a ɓoye kuma yadda ya kamata. Wannan yana ba ka damar ƙididdige Damage Modifier da waɗannan makamai bisa ga STR+DEX maimakon STR+SIZ, wanne ne ke haifar da kyakkyawan sakamako. Damage Modifier ba zai iya wuce ninki biyu na ƙimar lalacewar makami na yau da kullun ba. Alal misali, wuka tare da lalacewar 1d4+1, za a iyakance shi zuwa Damage Modifier na +1d4, yana sa shi 1d4+1+1d4. Wannan Ikon ya haɗa da kulob, wukake, garrotes, knives, shortswords, main gauche, da rapiers. Haka kuma harin hannu fanko.
